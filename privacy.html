Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What this app is

Mirror rclone is a private setup used by one family on a home smart mirror. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's permission, it reads and deletes image files in a single Google Drive folder called "Mirror Photos". It does not access any other files, email, contacts or other Google data.

How the data is used

Photos are copied to a Raspberry Pi in the family home and shown on the mirror. Photos older than seven days are removed from the mirror. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Only in the family's own Google Drive and on the Raspberry Pi in their home. Google sign-in tokens are stored on the Pi only.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
